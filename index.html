🌊 Waves & Sound Laboratory

Investigate transverse waves, longitudinal waves and sound. Adjust the wave properties and observe how the waveform changes.

Transverse Wave

A transverse wave oscillates perpendicular to the direction of propagation.

Calculated from v = fλ
Frequency 2 Hz
Amplitude 1
Wavelength 2 m
Speed 4 m/s
v = fλ
f = 1 / T
T = 1 / f
Increase frequency to produce more oscillations per second. Increase wavelength to increase the distance between successive crests.

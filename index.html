📘 Simple Series Circuit

In this virtual experiment, the battery provides electrical potential difference. When the switch is closed, the circuit becomes complete and current can flow through the resistor and bulb. According to Ohm's Law, the current is determined by the relationship V = IR. Increasing voltage increases current, while increasing resistance decreases current when the other variable is held constant.

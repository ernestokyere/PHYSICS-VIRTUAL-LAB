BUILD YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Physics concepts

Explore core principles and their mathematical relationships.

MECHANICS

Newton's second law

Net force equals mass multiplied by acceleration.

F = ma
ELECTRICITY

Ohm's law

For an ohmic conductor under constant physical conditions, voltage is proportional to current.

V = IR
WAVES

Wave speed

Wave speed is frequency multiplied by wavelength.

v = fλ
THERMAL PHYSICS

Thermal energy

For a temperature change without a phase change, heat energy can be calculated using specific heat capacity.

Q = mcΔT
OPTICS

Thin lens equation

Relates focal length to object and image distances using the standard real-is-positive convention.

1/f = 1/u + 1/v
ENERGY

Kinetic energy

The energy associated with the motion of an object.

Eₖ = ½mv²
